🎯 The Evaluation Criteria

Every concept was scored against these ten. A format only survives if it's scroll-stopping, repeatable, cheap, and impossible to separate from Lost Astronaut. Anything that only scored well on "virality" got cut — virality without defensibility just builds someone else's brand.

🔪 The Filtering Process

No mercy. Here's how 60+ became 20.

60+ raw concepts → ✂️ killed generic / cringe / expensive / un-repeatable → best 30 → 🏆 championship bracket → final 20, ranked

What got executed in the cull (and why):

💀 "Ask founders 5 questions" — every startup page already does it. Zero defensibility.

💀 Overproduced sci-fi sets / CGI control rooms — impossible to film weekly, and audiences smell budget-cosplay. The brand has to live in props and rules, not VFX.

💀 Motivational "chase your dreams" montages — cringe, un-ownable, attracts followers not builders.

💀 Plain "man on the street" — kept only the versions with a hard twist (a real offer, a real verdict, a real prop).

💀 One-off stunts — anything that couldn't credibly hit 50 episodes died on the spot.

🫵 The One to Test First

Start with 📡 Signal Found — not the bracket champion.

Here's the brutal-but-correct call. The Helmet wins the bracket because it's the most ownable format — but it's a destination, not a starting line. It needs a charismatic host with real authority, a helmet that doesn't look like a costume, and a roster of builders worth putting on trial. You don't have that roster yet.

Signal Found is the engine that builds everything else. It's the cheapest to produce, it's the literal mission ("find exceptional people before the world notices"), and every episode is you doing relationship-building on camera. The people you feature become the guest pipeline for The Helmet, Black Box, Ground Control, and Launch or Scrub. Test the engine first; the flagship comes online once you have fuel.

It's also the lowest-risk virality bet: "discovered before they blew up" is the single most re-shared format on the internet, and viewers screenshot it to plant a flag — "I saw them first." That's free distribution built into the format.

⚠️ Honest contingency: if within a week you land a magnetic host + a real helmet + two builders willing to be put on trial, run a Helmet pilot in parallel. It's the format you'll be remembered for — but Signal Found is the one that earns you the right to make it.

🗓️ 7-Day Execution Plan

Goal: ship the first batch — 3 Signal Found episodes + 1 Helmet pilot — by day 7. Two people, one camera, no studio.
